\subsection{What is verified, and what remains uncertain}
The saved accounting audit passed all recorded checks, including 286,920 transition records, 780 scalar-reference cases, and all five customer assignments. Its maximum cross-policy return reconstruction discrepancy was $3.64\times10^{-12}$. The report-building audit checks frozen identity, source hashes, assignment consistency, and table extraction without reopening market data or reevaluating policies.

The strengths are consistent real-wealth accounting, scenario-level separation, and explainable suitability and trading rules. However, synthetic paths and only 60 final observations limit external and tail-risk conclusions. Annual observation misses within-year losses. Strategic risk excludes fees and differs from realized policy risk; bill weights can drift below targets. Human response, withdrawals, taxes, and additional frictions are unmodeled. The cost sweep tests fees, not these other assumptions. Improvements require a new development protocol and independent evaluation data.
