\section{Recommendation and Conclusions}
We recommend the frozen calibrated Plan A. Conservative uses the lowest reference risk budget and a strategic bill floor of 40\%; Balanced uses an intermediate budget; Growth uses the highest budget and is eligible only for horizons of at least 15 years. Their horizon-specific allocations are given in Table~\ref{tab:weights}. Applying the suitability screen at each customer's horizon assigns B and C to Conservative, E to Balanced, and A and D to human review.

Balanced should use selective automated updates when estimated benefit exceeds transaction costs and the safety margin. At 30 years and the base fee, this cuts mean real fees by \CostSaving\% relative to annual rebalancing to the same anchor, with similar P90 drawdown. Against equal weight, median growth is higher but tail drawdown is worse. This supports cost-aware execution for suitable customers, without establishing universal risk or return superiority.

The main safeguard combines refusal at onboarding with suspension of automatic trades when observed real drawdown reaches the training-derived review threshold or model scores are invalid. Existing assets remain invested pending human review. For 30-year Balanced, the threshold is \ReviewThreshold\%, and the severe path still loses \SevereLoss\% from peak to trough. Escalation therefore cannot be presented as capital protection.
